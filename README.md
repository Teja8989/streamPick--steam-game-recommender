streamPick_steam_game_recommender

My app helps a Twitch or YouTube streamer decide which Steam game to play next by comparing player
activity, average playtime, and positive user reviews.

> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business (Fall 2026).

## Problem
Who your user is, what decision they have to make, and why it's hard without your app. (2-3 sentences)
The user is a yt streamer and they can choose which game to play by comparing stats. Without my app youtube gamers or twitch streamers would have to scroll through a bunch of games with no knowledge of whats trending.

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Sub-question 1:Which Steam games have the highest peak number of players online at
the same time?
2. **Sub-question 2:** Which games have the longest average playtime per owner? 
3. **Sub-question 3:** Among games with at least 1,000 user reviews, which have the highest
percentage of positive reviews

## Data
The [Steam Games Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset) on Kaggle, collected in March 2025. The dataset is shared under the MIT License by its creator, artermiloff. This repo uses `steam_games.csv`, a smaller file made for the course: the 1,000 games with the most reviews.
